01. The Problem

The Agony of Indian Hospital Waiting Rooms

2–4 Hrs

Blind Waiting

Patients travel 30+ km from villages and sit on crowded benches with zero visibility into when the doctor will call them.

70% Bharat

Tier-2/3 Neglect

Metro apps like Practo focus on expensive scheduled appointments, completely ignoring walk-in OPD queues in small towns.

Hazard

Cross-Infection & Noise

Packed reception lobbies increase airborne viral transmission, doctor fatigue, and receptionist burnout.

02. The Solution

Live In-Room Consultation Radar

📡 Live Token Consultation Radar

Patients see the exact token currently inside the doctor's room in real-time on their mobile (e.g. Now Serving #14 | Your Token #18).

🎟️ 10-Second Digital Token Booking

Book verified OPD passes with phone & email OTP, eliminating the need to physically stand in early morning queue lines.

💻 Zero Hardware Capital Cost

Zero ₹1.5L kiosk machines needed. Clinics run the entire caller system directly from any laptop, mobile, or tablet browser.

📍 Hyperlocal Town Radar

Instant sub-100ms OpenStreetMap location detection tailored specifically for district headquarters like Mahabubabad.
